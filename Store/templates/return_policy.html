Return & Refund Policy

Transparent 7-Day replacement and customer satisfaction guarantee across Pakistan.

7-Day Free Replacement Guarantee

All electronic modules, microcontrollers, and DIY kits are pre-tested before dispatch. We offer free replacements for defective or damaged items.

1. Return & Replacement Window

You may initiate a return or replacement request within 7 days of receiving your package. If 7 days have passed since delivery, please contact our engineering support team for troubleshooting assistance.

2. Eligibility for Returns & Replacements

To qualify for a replacement or return:

  • The item must be in its original packaging with all included accessories (headers, cables, manuals).
  • For transit damage or missing components, please provide an unboxing photo/video or proof of delivery.
  • Pre-tested microcontrollers and sensors damaged by user incorrect wiring, overvoltage, or short-circuits are not eligible for standard returns but can be diagnosed by our tech team.

3. Refunds (Cash on Delivery & Online)

Once your returned item is received and inspected at our Lahore fulfillment center:

  • Replacements: Dispatched immediately within 24 hours at zero additional shipping cost.
  • Refunds: Processed within 2–3 business days directly to your Bank Account, JazzCash, or EasyPaisa wallet.

4. Return Shipping Process

To start a return:

  1. Message our customer support on WhatsApp at +92 310 4505008 or email support@projectstore.pk with your Order ID.
  2. Pack the item safely and send it via any courier (TCS, Leopards, PostEx, M&P) to our Lahore fulfillment address:
    2nd Floor, 72, Westend Plaza, Shahrah-e-Quaid-e-Azam, Mozang Chungi, Lahore, 54000, Pakistan
  3. If the return is due to a defective or incorrect item, ProjectStore will reimburse the return courier charges.

5. Need Immediate Help?

Our engineering support team is active Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM PKT.
